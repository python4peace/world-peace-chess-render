Rules, privacy, and free-hosting limits

Host plays White; Black joins with a private invitation. Clocks start when Black clicks Join. Against Stockfish, White clicks Start clock. No undo or pause. Clocks continue during disconnections and network delay. Refresh on this same browser and address to reconnect.

Server restart, redeploy or Render sleep deletes rooms. Rooms expire after 24 hours. Keep a PGN copy. This is casual play, not tournament-certified. Timeout draws use a material-based approximation. Draw claims are for the current position only; intended-next-move claims and draw offers are not implemented.

Never post a private opponent invitation or your HOST_CODE on TikTok. Use the clean spectator link. Anyone with the invitation can control Black. Browser storage holds your seat, so do not clear it midgame. No accounts, ratings, anti-cheat or chat voting. Internet is needed for the chess.js browser library.
